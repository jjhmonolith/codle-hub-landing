## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Codle is an institutional AI education product hub that presents six distinct offerings spanning class operation, content and educational materials, intensive education, and project-based events.

**What it does:** The hub helps teachers and institutional buyers discover Codle's product range beyond the software license. It keeps all six offerings visible at the top level, explains required purchase relationships, and routes each offering to the correct sales path.

**Product category:** Institutional AI education product portfolio / product hub

**Product type:** Mixed portfolio: SaaS license, paid content access, publisher-sold educational materials, and institution-contracted education programs.

**Business model:**
- Directly sold by Team Monolith/Codle: 코들 라이선스, 코들 오리지널 콘텐츠, 씨마스 인공지능 기초, AI 집중캠프, 바이브코딩 해커톤.
- Publisher-sold: AIDT 교육자료. Each publisher sells and collects payment; the current hub must not use a Codle quotation CTA for this offering.
- Public prices and discounts are not approved for the hub.

## Target Audience

**Target organizations:** Schools, education offices, and education-related institutions considering AI/information classes, content, educational materials, intensive programs, or project events.

**Observed buying roles:**
- User/champion: 담당교사
- Reviewer: 부장·관리자
- Contract/budget role: 행정실·기관 담당자
- Program decision roles may vary by institution. Public persona claims require further approval.

**Primary use case:** Discover the relevant Codle offering and determine the correct next action without assuming Codle only sells classroom software.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Compare available AI education offerings by the educational situation being planned.
- Understand which offerings can be reviewed together and which require a prerequisite.
- Route direct-sold offerings to one multi-select inquiry and AIDT 교육자료 to publisher guidance.

**Use cases:**
- 수업 운영 도구 검토
- AI 교육 콘텐츠·교육자료 검토
- 집중 교육 프로그램 검토
- 프로젝트형 행사·해커톤 검토

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Existing customer perception may stop at Codle as classroom software, so other approved offerings are not discovered.

**Why the current presentation falls short:** A visually dramatic promise without naming the actual portfolio forces visitors to infer what Codle now offers.

**What it costs them:** Not approved for public quantitative or outcome claims.

**Emotional tension:** Not sufficiently evidenced for public copy. Do not invent anxiety or urgency.

## Competitive Landscape

No competitor claims are approved. Do not name or compare competitors.

**Status quo for this project:** Visitors may arrive with a narrow software-only perception. The page must broaden perception through concrete offering names rather than attacks on alternatives.

## Differentiation

**Approved strategic distinction:** Six separate top-level offerings can be discovered in one hub, while their commercial relationships remain accurate.

**How we do it differently:**
- Discovery hierarchy and purchase hierarchy are separated.
- All six offerings remain visible.
- Direct-sold five offerings support one multi-select inquiry.
- AIDT 교육자료 uses publisher guidance instead of a Codle quote path.

**Why that's better:** This is an internal design rationale, not an approved public outcome claim.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Existing software-only perception limits discovery of the broader portfolio.

**Pull:** Concrete visibility of six offerings and clear purchase routing.

**Habit:** Visitors may look only for license/pricing information.

**Anxiety:** No approved customer-language evidence. Do not generate switching-fear copy.

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Professional, affirmative, concrete, calm.

**Style:** Lead with named educational situations and product types. Use short Korean sentences. Prefer factual portfolio language over abstract transformation language.

**Personality:** Clear, capable, understated, institution-ready.

## Proof Points

**Approved facts:**
- Six top-level offerings exist and are currently available through their respective sales paths.
- Five offerings are directly sold by Team Monolith/Codle and support one multi-select inquiry.
- Content offerings require Pro.
- AIDT 교육자료 follows publisher sales guidance.

**Do not use:** Pending prices, discounts, institution counts, adoption rates, certifications, partner logos, testimonials, performance metrics, or unapproved content lists.

## Goals

**Business goal:** Expand customer perception from software-only to a discoverable AI education portfolio and create cross-offering inquiry opportunities.

**Primary conversion action:** `여섯 상품 보기`

**Secondary conversion:** `내부 검토용 견적 문의` for the five directly sold offerings.

**AIDT action:** Publisher guidance; do not include it in the Codle quotation tray.
