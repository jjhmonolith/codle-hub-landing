# Codle Brand Context — 상품 허브

**Version:** v1
**Updated:** 2026-08-26

## Brand positioning for this page

Codle should be understood not only as classroom software, but as a concrete portfolio of six institutional AI education offerings. This is an internal positioning objective; public copy must prove it by naming offerings rather than claiming `AI 교육의 전 범위`.

## Primary audience

Teachers and institutional decision participants who are considering an AI class, content or educational material, an intensive program, or a project-based event.

## Key benefit

Visitors can discover six distinct offerings in one place and follow the correct purchase path for each.

## Message constraints

- `AIDT 교육자료` is the official name; expanded form is `AI 디지털 교육자료`.
- Keep AI 집중캠프 and 바이브코딩 해커톤 as separate top-level offerings.
- Keep 코들 오리지널 콘텐츠 and 씨마스 인공지능 기초 visible at top level, but state that Pro is required.
- Direct-sold five offerings may be selected in one inquiry.
- AIDT 교육자료 must route to publisher guidance, not a Codle quotation CTA.
- Do not publish price figures, discounts, institution counts, adoption rates, certifications, logos, testimonials, or outcome claims without approval.

## Voice

- Affirmative, not defensive.
- Concrete before clever.
- Product and educational situation names before abstract brand language.
- Short, direct Korean.
- Avoid `단순한 X가 아니라 Y`, `모든 것`, `전 범위`, `통합 솔루션`, `혁신`, `미래 교육`.

## Current issue to solve

Concept A is the preferred direction, but `AI 교육의 다음 선택까지` and its supporting narrative do not tell a first-time visitor quickly enough what Codle now offers. The revised hero must name the range, and the page must move from educational situation → relevant offerings → purchase route.
